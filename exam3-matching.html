How to use this activity

Each round has 7 or 8 clues and the same number of terms. For each clue, choose the matching term from its menu. Each term is used once per round, and terms you have already chosen are marked “used.”

Check answers marks each clue correct or not, so you can fix the ones you missed. Show answers fills in the rest of the round. Move between rounds with Previous round and Next round, or jump to any round from the list at the bottom.

Mixed rounds shuffles all the terms into new groups; choose New mix for a different shuffle. By topic groups related terms together, which makes them harder to tell apart. Your progress is saved in this browser when possible.
